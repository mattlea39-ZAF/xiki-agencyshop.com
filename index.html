Why implant enquiries go quiet

They didn't say no. They said not yet.

Most practices are busy enough with new enquiries that the old ones get one follow-up call, then silence. That is the goldmine sitting in your practice software.

⏳

Timing, not interest

Holidays, money, nerves. The patient was interested when they enquired and nothing about that has changed, except that nobody asked again.

💷

The price needed a second conversation

A four-figure implant plan rarely gets accepted on the spot. The patients who went quiet after the quote are the easiest ones to bring back.

📞

Nobody has time to chase

Reception is answering the phone and the TCO is in surgery. A friendly text conversation does the chasing, and only the live ones reach your team.

Questions dentists ask

Straight answers.

Is this allowed under GDPR?

These are people who contacted your practice about treatment, so following up on their own enquiry is a legitimate interest. Every message identifies the practice, and anyone who replies stop is never contacted again.

Will patients know it is AI?

The conversation is in your practice's name and reads like a receptionist checking in. When a patient asks something the AI should not answer, it hands over to your team rather than guessing.

What does it cost?

You pay per booked appointment. There is no set-up fee, no retainer and no ad spend, so if nobody books you pay nothing.

Do we need to change our software?

No. We start from an export of the patients who never booked. Bookings go into your existing diary the way your team already uses it.

How quickly do we see results?

Conversations start within days of the export and the first consultations usually land inside the first week.
